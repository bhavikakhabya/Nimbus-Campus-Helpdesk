# Hostel Rules (Mock Assignment Document)

This is fictional sample content created only for the Nimbus Campus Helpdesk assignment. It is not an official university policy.

1. Residents must carry their campus ID when entering the hostel.
2. Visitors must register at the reception desk.
3. Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
4. Residents should keep rooms and shared areas clean.
5. Report maintenance or safety concerns to the hostel office.
6. Do not share room keys or access cards.
